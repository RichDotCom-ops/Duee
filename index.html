FAQ

Common questions

Is duee. really free? ▾
Yes — the Free plan has no time limit and no credit card required. You get all the core features plus 20 AI messages per day. Upgrade to Pro if you want unlimited AI.
What's an AI message? ▾
Every message you send to the ✦ Study Assistant counts as one. Free users get 20 per day (resets every 24 hours). Pro users get unlimited.
How does Canvas import work? ▾
Export your course calendar from Canvas or Google Classroom as a .ics file, then drag it into duee. All your assignments are imported automatically — no manual entry.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription? ▾
Yes, cancel anytime. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. No penalties, no questions asked.
Is my data private? ▾
Your data is stored securely in Supabase with row-level security — only you can access your assignments and classes. We never sell your data.
