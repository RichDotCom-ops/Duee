Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before upgrading.
What counts as an AI message? ▾
Every message you send to the AI assistant (the ✦ button) uses one message. Free users get 10 per day — the counter resets every 24 hours automatically. Pro users have no limit.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Absolutely. Cancel any time and you'll keep Pro access through the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
Is my data safe? ▾
Yes. Your classes and assignments are stored in Supabase with row-level security — only you can see your data. We never sell or share your information with third parties.
What happens if I go back to Free? ▾
All your classes, assignments, and history stay intact — nothing is deleted. You'll just return to the 10 AI messages per day limit.
How is the Annual plan so much cheaper? ▾
Paying upfront for a full year lets us offer a massive 77% discount vs monthly. At $40.99/year, that's about $3.42/month — less than one cup of coffee.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover). Apple Pay and Google Pay are also supported on compatible browsers.
